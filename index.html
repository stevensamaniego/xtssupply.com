About XTS Supply

XTS Supply LLC is a registered Texas LLC and professional e-commerce retail company headquartered in El Paso, Texas.

We purchase products from authorized wholesalers and distributors, then sell them to consumers through Amazon's Fulfilled by Amazon (FBA) program and other online channels. Our focus is on trusted, name-brand products across categories with strong consumer demand — grocery staples, health essentials, books, pet care, and home goods.

We hold a Texas Sales & Use Tax Permit and maintain full compliance with all marketplace and resale requirements. We're actively expanding our supplier network and product catalog to serve more customers across the country.

Our Approach

We treat every supplier relationship as a long-term partnership.

Consistent Ordering

We place regular, recurring orders — not one-time buys. We're looking for suppliers who want a reliable, long-term retail partner.

Professional Operations
